# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Three roles in one company: **Employee** (works remotely, walks up to teammates, meets, clocks in), **Manager** (also watches team attendance and the Command Center, sends announcements), **Admin** (manages people, teams, the office layout and integrations). Office laptops in a browser; English only for v1.

## Product Purpose

A 3D virtual office in the browser for a company moving to remote work. It gives the company one shared office online, with buildings, floors and rooms, so quick hallway talks, knowing who is around, and meeting rooms come back without sending a link for every conversation.

## Positioning

Talking starts by walking up to someone in a shared office, not by scheduling a call.

## Operating Context

- One company per deployment; the local seed is "Corridor Technologies" in New Cairo (HQ with 3 floors, plus the Innovation Lab).
- Daily rituals the product supports: punching in and out, meetings with booked rooms, town halls, a read-only Jira board.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- v1 scope and order: SPEC.md section 1.1 (F-00 to F-15).
- Out of scope for v1: activity monitoring and screenshots, self-service sign-up for other companies, mobile apps, meeting recording, editing floor layouts in the UI.
- Personal data (names, work emails, attendance, chat) under Egypt PDPL; admin actions are audited.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Corridor" (working name), wordmark and brand mark as defined in `ui/DESIGN.md`.
- Voice: plain, short, human sentences; error copy says what happened and what to do.

## Evidence on Hand

No customers, testimonials or metrics yet. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The office should feel like a place, not a dashboard.
2. Admin screens are for getting a task done quickly and safely; mistakes that lock people out are prevented, not just warned about.
3. Every screen works for keyboard and screen-reader users, in light and dark.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA; every screen in light and dark and at 390px wide.
